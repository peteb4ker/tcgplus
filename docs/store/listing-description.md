## Full description

TCGPlus helps you buy cards on TCGplayer for less. It adds the information you need to compare listings to the TCGplayer pages you already use, so you can find the best listing without opening each one or doing the math yourself.

Find the best listing:

- See whether a price is good. Every listing shows how far it is from market price for that exact printing and condition, colour-coded from green (below market) to red (10% or more over).
- Know what shipping will cost. Shipping is colour-coded so expensive shipping stands out.
- Spot real deals. A purple DEAL badge appears only when the price plus the shipping you'd actually pay is still below market. It knows TCGplayer charges one shipping fee per seller, so cards from a seller already in your cart count as shipping free.
- See where each seller ships from. Each listing shows the seller's city and state, highlighted if they're in your home state or a nearby state you choose.
- Filter by location. A small panel counts listings by location; click a group to show only those sellers.

Before you pay:

- Check whether your whole cart is worth it. On the cart and checkout pages, TCGPlus compares your total, with shipping and tax, against what the same cards would cost at market price, and shows the difference, for example "-$3.14 (-8.2%)". If it can't read part of your cart, it tells you instead of guessing.
- Track your spending. Your cart subtotal is shown next to the cart icon on every page.

Settings let you:

- Choose your home state and any number of nearby states.
- Hide parts of the product page you don't use: the price breakdown, the recommendations carousel and the footer.
- Always search for Near Mint, so you don't have to set the condition filter each time.
- Hide search results that have no listings matching your filters. TCGplayer marks these "Out of Stock", but usually they're just in another language or condition.

Privacy:

- TCGPlus runs entirely in your browser. There's no TCGPlus server, no account and no analytics. Your settings stay on your device.
- It only talks to TCGplayer, to look up seller locations, your cart subtotal and market prices. Only the cart request uses your TCGplayer session; the others are sent without cookies.
- It's open source under the MIT license. Source, issues and release notes: https://github.com/peteb4ker/tcgplus

TCGPlus is an independent tool. It isn't affiliated with, endorsed by or certified by TCGplayer, Inc. "TCGplayer" and the TCGplayer logo are trademarks of TCGplayer, Inc. TCGPlus only adds information to the page and reads what's already there. It doesn't scrape, doesn't click or buy anything for you, and doesn't change how TCGplayer works beyond the settings you control.
