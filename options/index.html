About TCGPlus

TCGPlus is an independent, open-source browser extension. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or certified by TCGplayer, Inc. "TCGplayer" and the TCGplayer logo are trademarks of TCGplayer, Inc.

How your data flows. TCGPlus runs entirely in your browser and uses your existing TCGplayer session. It fetches vendor information and your cart subtotal from TCGplayer's own endpoints, reads market prices from the page you're viewing, and stores your settings locally in chrome.storage.local. Nothing is sent to any third party. There is no TCGPlus server.

What TCGPlus does on the page. TCGPlus only adds visual overlays and reads what's already on the page. It doesn't scrape, doesn't automate clicks or purchases, and doesn't change how TCGplayer's site behaves beyond the toggles you control in settings.

Source & issues. TCGPlus is MIT-licensed. Source code, bug reports, and feature requests: github.com/peteb4ker/tcgplus. See the privacy policy for the full data-flow description.
